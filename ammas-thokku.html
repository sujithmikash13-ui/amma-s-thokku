Rooted in South India. Made for everywhere.

Thokku has always travelled well — packed into steel tiffins, carried to hostels and new cities, kept on a shelf for whenever rice needed something more. Amma's Thokku is made in that same spirit: for wherever you happen to be living now, and whatever's closest to home when you eat it.
